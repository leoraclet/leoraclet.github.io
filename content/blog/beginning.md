## Welcome to my blog !

As a first post, I would like to start by explaining why I'm making this blog.

<!--more-->

### Why ?

I've always loved to learn about anything and everything, but what I actually enjoy even more than learning is "teaching" others, or at least telling them about what I've learned that is completely surprising or totally new to me. Every time I discover something, I'm so excited to talk about it and share what I've found.

For most of my life, I only stored this knowledge in my head because I'm good at it, and I'm convinced that when you truly understand things, it is easier to memorize. This method has worked for a long time and still does.

However, during the last year, as I learned even more than ever before in cybersecurity, I started to feel the need to store that knowledge somewhere in a comprehensible way.

**And here it is!**

### What you'll find

I will use this blog mainly to share my knowledge with other people by posting articles about things I’ve learned (recently or not), projects I’ve worked on, and sometimes write-ups for CTF challenges I solved.

The content of this blog will be mainly about **cryptography** and **steganography**, but you will also find some **binary exploitation** (pwn) and **reverse engineering**, as those are my favorite domains in IT. There will sometimes be posts or projects about completely different topics.

Happy reading, and I hope you'll learn something while reading my work !
