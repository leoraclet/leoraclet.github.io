The [**Edwards-curve Digital Signature Algorithm**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EdDSA) (EdDSA) is a digital signature scheme using a variant of [Schnorr signature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schnorr_signature) based on [twisted Edwards curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twisted_Edwards_curve).
